Data Collection and Usage

Video Processing

LastFrame performs all video-to-photo extraction locally on your device. Your video files and extracted images are not transmitted to our servers and never leave your device during the processing stage. The app functions entirely offline, meaning it does not require an internet connection to operate. All processed content remains under your sole control in your local storage, and you retain the right to delete these files at any time via the application or your device’s file manager. We do not access, track, or store your personal media content.

Photo Enhancement

The app features an optional cloud-based enhancement tool that uses AI to improve image quality. If you choose to use this feature, the specific image is sent to our servers over an active internet connection for processing. Once the enhancement is completed, the resulting file is downloaded back to your device, and the source/output images are permanently deleted from our servers within one hour. We do not use your personal images to train AI models, nor do we share them with third parties beyond the necessary processing infrastructure.
